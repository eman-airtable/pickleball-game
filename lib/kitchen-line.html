Rules on this court

  • Two bounces. The serve and the return each have to bounce before anyone volleys.
  • The kitchen. The 7-foot zone on each side of the net. Volley from inside it and you lose the point. A dink that lands in it is legal and awkward to return.
  • Side-out scoring. Only the server scores. Lose a rally as server and the serve passes over.
  • Serving. An even score serves from the right court, odd from the left, always to the diagonal box beyond the kitchen.
  • Power meter. Hold Drive or Dink and a ring fills around your player. Release in the yellow band for a fast, accurate shot. Hold into the red and the shot sails long. A quick tap hits a normal shot.
  • Online. Press Play online and create a game to get a four-letter room code, then send it or the invite link to a friend. You both see yourselves at the bottom of the court. Pausing pauses the game for both of you.
  • Spin. Release, then brush sideways while you swing. Sweep left and the ball heads right, sweep right and it heads left. It lands that way of your aim, then kicks further that way off the bounce. Overdo it and it goes wide. A spun ball is harder to read and harder to return.

Controls

Move
Mouse, finger drag, or WASD / arrows
Drive
Hold click, Space or Z to charge, release to hit
Dink
Hold right-click or X to charge, release to hit
Aim
Meet the ball with the left or right side of your paddle. The dashed ring is your reach and brightens when the ball is in range.
Spin
Release a shot, then brush sideways right away with A or D, a drag, or a quick mouse swipe. Brush left and the ball goes right. Brush right and it goes left. Auto swing never adds spin.
Touch
On a phone, touch the left side of the court and a stick appears under your thumb: push to move, further for faster. Hold Drive or Dink to charge and release to swing, or just tap for a normal shot. Auto swing hits a decent shot for you when the ball is in reach. Hold a button to take over.
